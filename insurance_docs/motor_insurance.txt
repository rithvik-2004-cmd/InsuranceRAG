
DOCUMENT: Sample Motor Insurance Policy
DOCUMENT TYPE: Motor Insurance
POLICY ID: MOTOR-001
NOTICE: Fictional sample policy for RAG learning only.

1. Purpose of Motor Insurance
Motor insurance provides financial protection for specified vehicle-related risks, subject to the selected coverage and policy conditions.

2. Third-Party Liability
Third-party liability coverage may cover the insured person's legal liability for injury to another person or damage to another person's property, subject to the applicable policy terms.

3. Own-Damage Coverage
Own-damage coverage may cover specified damage to the insured vehicle resulting from covered events. The actual events covered depend on the selected policy and its exclusions.

4. Accidental Damage
Accidental damage refers to damage caused by an accident. Whether repairs are payable depends on the coverage selected, the circumstances of the accident, applicable deductibles, and policy exclusions.

5. Theft
Vehicle theft claims are assessed according to the policy terms and required documentation. The insurer may require a police report, claim form, vehicle records, and other supporting documents.

6. Deductible
A deductible is the portion of an eligible claim that the policyholder must bear before the insurer pays the applicable amount. The actual deductible depends on the policy terms.

7. Claim Notification
The policyholder should notify the insurer promptly after an accident, theft, or other potentially covered event. The insurer may provide instructions for inspection, repairs, and documentation.

8. Repair Authorization
The policyholder should follow the insurer's claim instructions before proceeding with repairs. Whether a repair is authorized or payable depends on the policy and claim assessment.

9. Exclusions
A motor insurance policy may exclude certain events, uses, damages, or circumstances. The actual exclusions must be checked in the policy wording.

10. No-Claim Bonus
A no-claim bonus may reward eligible policyholders for claim-free periods. Eligibility and the applicable benefit depend on the insurer's rules and policy terms.
