INSURANCE POLICY: HEALTH COVERAGE

1. Eligibility
This health insurance policy is available to individuals aged 18 to 65 years.

2. Premium
The premium is the amount paid by the policyholder to maintain insurance coverage. Premiums may vary based on age, coverage amount, and selected benefits.

3. Hospitalization Coverage
The policy covers eligible hospitalization expenses, subject to policy terms, exclusions, and coverage limits.

4. Waiting Period
Certain illnesses and treatments may have a waiting period before coverage becomes available. The applicable waiting period is specified in the policy schedule.

5. Deductible
A deductible is the amount the policyholder must pay for covered expenses before the insurer starts paying the applicable amount.

6. Exclusions
Expenses explicitly excluded under the policy are not covered. Policyholders should review the exclusions before purchasing the policy.

7. Claims
To submit a claim, the policyholder must provide the required claim form, medical bills, discharge summary, and other documents requested by the insurer.

8. Policy Limit
The policy limit is the maximum amount payable by the insurer for covered expenses under the applicable policy terms.
